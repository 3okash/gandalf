Gandalf's - McMinnville

Gandalf's Smoke & Vape Shop, located in McMinnville, Oregon, is a well-known smoke shop destination for smokers and vapers since 2013. Located in Oregon's wine country; approximately 45 minutes from downtown Portland. Conveniently located on highway 99, we serve up caring customer service and a wide variety of smoking and vaping essentials.
